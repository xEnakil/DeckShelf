Deck Shelf third-party notices
==============================

The native application is built from Rust crates recorded exactly in Cargo.lock.
The principal UI and windowing libraries are:

- Wry 0.56.1 (Apache-2.0 OR MIT)
- Tao 0.36.0 (Apache-2.0 OR MIT)
- Serde and serde_json (Apache-2.0 OR MIT)
- image (MIT OR Apache-2.0)
- open (MIT)

Cargo.lock is the authoritative inventory of direct and transitive source
dependencies for a release. Each crate's source distribution contains its
copyright and license terms. Deck Shelf does not bundle an Electron, Node.js,
WebView2, WKWebView, WebKitGTK, or Visual C++ runtime. Windows and macOS use the
webview supplied by the operating system. Linux uses the system WebKitGTK 4.1
library.
